Hold your headset's voice-command button to rewind active media. Default: 30 seconds, adjustable from 1 to 120 seconds.

Grant Notification access, save a duration, start a seekable player, then hold the headset button and choose Headset Rewind. On Android 13+, Allow restricted settings in system App info may be needed first.

Requires Android 9+ and a headset that launches voice commands. Single/double/triple clicks are unchanged. Calls, ads, live streams, and player restrictions can prevent seeking.

Notification contents are unused. No network, microphone, Accessibility access, ads, analytics, or runtime libraries.
